Salon Ledger for Hostready

How to switch on the selling version, add customer salons, collect renewals and control devices.

Do once · about 5 minutes

1. Upgrade the database

  1. In Supabase, open SQL Editor → New query.
  2. Open upgrade-v2-multi-salon.sql, copy all of it, paste, and press Run. You should see “Salon Ledger v2 database upgrade complete”.
  3. Go to Authentication → Sign In / Providers:
    • Turn on Allow new users to sign up. Only emails you or a salon owner have added can actually sign up; everyone else is refused by the database.
    • Turn off Confirm email, so staff can start straight after choosing a password.
  4. Install the new app version on your devices.
Order matters: run the SQL before using the new app. Older app versions (1.0.2 and earlier) stop syncing after the upgrade, so update every device.

Your existing records become salon SALON-0001 “My Salon” (Premium, 10 devices, paid for 365 days). Your login becomes its owner and the Hostready admin. Rename it in the Admin tab.

Each new customer · 2 minutes

2. Add a customer salon

  1. Open Hostready Admin → New salon.
  2. Enter the salon name, plan, devices included, trial days (default 7), and the owner's name and email. Leave the Salon Code blank to create one automatically.
  3. Send the owner:
    • the download link: github.com/hostreadyllc/Saloon-Spa/releases/latest
    • their Salon Code
    • this instruction: “Open the app, enter the Salon Code, tap ‘First time? Activate your account’, use your email and choose a password.”
  4. The owner's first device gets 2 days of temporary access and they approve it in Settings → Devices. They add their own staff in Settings → Team logins.
Subscriptions

3. Trials, renewals and expiry

StageWhat the salon sees
Free trial (7 days)Blue banner: days left in the trial and your contact details.
7 days before the endYellow banner: “Your subscription ends on … Renew to avoid interruption.”
After the end dateRed banner. Read-only: they can view and export, but cannot add or change anything. The database enforces this, so changing the device clock doesn't help.
Suspended (by you)Same as expired, with “account suspended”.

When a salon pays: Admin → Manage → Record payment & extend. Choose 90, 180, 270 or 365 days, enter the amount excluding VAT and press Record. The new period starts the day after the current one ends, a Hostready tax invoice number is created (HR-YYMM-0001…), and the PDF downloads. Every device unlocks within a few minutes.

Fill in Hostready details (phone, email, TRN, address, bank details) in the Admin tab. They appear on renewal banners and on your invoices.

Devices

4. Device control

  • Every phone, tablet or PC registers itself when someone signs in and gets 2 days of temporary access. At most 2 devices per salon can be on temporary access at once.
  • The owner can approve devices up to the plan's limit. Beyond the limit they see “Your plan includes N devices. Contact Hostready to add more.”
  • To sell an extra device: Admin → Manage → raise Devices included and save (or approve that device directly there).
  • Removing a device locks it out immediately, for example when a phone is lost or a staff member leaves.
  • An unapproved or removed device sees no data at all. This is checked by the database, not just the app.
Updates

5. Changing the software later

  • All salons share one app. When you ask Claude for a change, it goes to GitHub, the installers rebuild automatically, and the new version appears at the download link.
  • Windows updates itself in the background. Android shows an “Update” button once the permanent signing key secrets are added in GitHub. iPhone/iPad (web version) updates on its own.
  • Salon data lives in the cloud, so updating or reinstalling never deletes records.
  • Each salon's services, prices, staff, receipt details and opening hours are settings inside the app. They never need a new build.
Before your first paying customer

6. Checklist

  • Upgrade Supabase to a paid plan for daily backups and no pausing.
  • Add the 2 Android signing key secrets in GitHub (Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions).
  • Fill in Hostready details in the Admin tab.
  • Optional: a code-signing certificate, which removes the “unknown publisher” warning on Windows and Mac.
  • Have your terms of service and privacy policy ready, because you store your customers' client data.
